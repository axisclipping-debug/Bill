Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about how the 60-minute executive session works? Here are the answers.

Is this audit really free? What is the catch?

Yes, it is entirely free. The audit is designed to deliver immediate diagnostic value. By showcasing how we find and fix leadership bottlenecks in real-time, some companies later choose to hire Bill to consult on their operations. There is zero obligation or sales pitch required to get your action roadmap.

Why do you restrict applications to companies with 10–250 employees?

Companies within this size range face specific scaling friction points: moving from founder-driven execution to management systems. Teams with fewer than 10 employees do not have complex alignment challenges, and companies with more than 250 employees require enterprise governance reviews that cannot be resolved in a single 60-minute session.

What preparation is required before our session?

Minimal preparation. Once approved, you will receive a quick 4-question onboarding form to outline your current reporting structure and key team deliverables. You will also want to make sure your core leadership team members (if joining) are available for the full 60 minutes.

How long does it take to get our scorecard and action roadmap?

You leave the actual 60-minute call with your primary action roadmap completed. Bill will compile your formalized Leadership Clarity Scorecard PDF and deliver it to your inbox within 24 hours of the audit session.
